## Expansion guidance

Prioritize sparse experiential identities and underrepresented moods/interaction patterns, then source-backed neighbors for isolated titles. Add several diverse records per new cluster rather than a lone exemplar. Review template-derived editorial profiles: repeated metadata can create dense components and artificially high overlap. Expand beyond dominant Exploration/Narrative-driven profiles. Regenerate frequencies, components, empty-mode counts and five regression cases before expansion; do not change weights to hide gaps.

Frequencies describe recorded editorial labels, not the historical distribution of games. A zero-use value may reflect a tagging gap. Detailed nodes/components are in discovery-coverage.json.
